Etsy Photo Organizer

Stop spending hours sorting product photos by hand. Point it at a messy folder and it files every photo under the real Etsy listing it belongs to.

Download for Windows

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Mac App Store version coming soon.

What it does

  • Connects to your real Etsy shop and reads your actual listing titles and tags
  • Matches each photo in a folder to the specific listing it belongs to -- not just a category guess
  • Handles the photos your camera named. IMG_4471.png has nothing to match on, so it gets compared against the photos that did match and filed with the product it actually looks like
  • Flags anything it isn't confident about for you to review by hand, instead of filing it wrong
  • Files everything into real, named folders automatically, and every run can be undone in one click

Try it before connecting anything

The app ships with a sample shop. Click “Try it with sample photos” on the Organize screen and it runs the real thing — no Etsy account needed, no folders to add. Most of the sample photos are named the way a camera names them, so you can see exactly how that case is handled before trusting it with your own files.
