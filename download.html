Etsy Photo Organizer

Stop spending hours sorting product photos by hand. Point it at a messy folder and it files every photo under the real Etsy listing it belongs to.

Download for Windows

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Mac App Store version coming soon.

What it does

  • Connects to your real Etsy shop and reads your actual listing titles and tags
  • Matches each photo in a folder to the specific listing it belongs to -- not just a category guess
  • Flags anything it isn't confident about for you to review by hand, instead of filing it wrong
  • Files everything into real, named folders automatically
