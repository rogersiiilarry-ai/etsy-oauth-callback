Etsy Photo Organizer -- Support

Etsy Photo Organizer helps an Etsy seller organize their own product photos, using their own real past listing history and on-device photo analysis -- nothing uploaded, no fabricated results.

Need help?

Email organizersupport@gmail.com and describe what happened. Include your macOS version and, if something didn't work as expected, what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Why is a photo marked "Unsorted"? The app only sorts a photo into a category when it finds a real match -- either the filename or the photo's own content matches a tag or word you've actually used in a past listing. If your shop has no listings yet, or a photo doesn't match anything, it's placed in Unsorted rather than guessed at.

Does this edit or publish anything on Etsy? No. The app only reads your shop's existing listings to learn your patterns. It never creates, edits, or publishes a listing.

Where are my photos actually moved to? Only folders you explicitly add, and only when you click "File these into folders" and choose a destination yourself. Nothing happens automatically in the background.
